# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public-facing MARSA yacht-booking experience and any web-exported consumer routes in `artifacts/marsa-mobile`.
- Public static HTML, crawlability, metadata, social previews, structured data, and source-detectable accessibility.

## Out of scope
- Authenticated MARSA Admin dashboard (`artifacts/marsa-admin`) and its operational routes.
- Native-only mobile screens, API endpoints, and internal mockup/canvas tooling unless they expose a public web surface.

## Target audience
- Guests searching for yacht rentals and boat experiences in Egypt.
- Yacht hosts may use the product, but host/admin operational screens are not public search content.

## Primary keywords
- Unknown; product positioning indicates yacht booking, yacht rental, and boat trips in Egypt.

## Crawler assumptions
- Public web routes should be discoverable by Googlebot, social preview bots, and AI crawlers from static HTML where the web export supports it.
- Authenticated booking and management flows should not be indexed as public content.

- None yet.
